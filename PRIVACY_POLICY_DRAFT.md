# CartSense privacy policy draft

Last updated: August 17, 2026

CartSense helps users scan grocery receipts, create shopping lists and understand household grocery spending.

## Information we process

CartSense may process:

- Account information such as name and email when a user signs in.
- Receipt photos selected or captured by the user.
- Extracted receipt details such as store name, date, products, categories, prices and totals.
- Shopping-list items, budgets, family profile preferences and reminder settings.
- Anonymous aggregated product and pricing signals for improving grocery insights.

## How we use information

We use information to:

- Read and save grocery bills.
- Build shopping and price insights.
- Support login, backup and sync features.
- Improve scanning accuracy and category classification.
- Generate anonymous aggregated FMCG and pricing analytics.

## Anonymous analytics

CartSense may use aggregated anonymous grocery trends such as product category, store, price, quantity and time period. These reports should not include names, phone numbers, emails, exact addresses or receipt images.

## Data sharing

CartSense does not sell personal user data. If business analytics are offered to partners, they should be aggregated and anonymous.

## User control

Users can continue using key features in guest mode. Users may delete local app data by uninstalling the app. Account/cloud deletion options should be provided before public production launch.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the CartSense owner or support email that will be published in the Play Store listing.
