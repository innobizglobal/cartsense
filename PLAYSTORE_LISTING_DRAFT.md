# CartSense Play Store listing draft

## App title

CartSense Grocery Saver

## Short description

Scan grocery bills, plan shopping lists and track household spending.

## Full description

CartSense helps families manage grocery shopping in a simple way.

Scan a supermarket bill and CartSense reads the products, prices and bill total. Build a shopping list before going to the store, tick items while shopping and compare the final receipt after checkout.

What CartSense helps with:

- Scan grocery receipts
- Save recent bills
- Track monthly grocery spend
- See category-wise spending
- Build a simple shopping list
- Add products by typing or voice
- Handle long receipts with multiple photos
- Review planned, purchased, missed and extra items
- Use Hindi and Telugu language options
- Sign in for account-based features and future cloud sync

CartSense is designed for everyday household grocery use. It keeps the workflow simple: plan, shop, scan and review.

Privacy note: receipt scanning may use secure AI processing when enabled. Private on-device scanning remains available where supported.
